Out of black ink but need to print a shipping label or document? The tool above converts a PDF or image to a dark ink color of your choice so your printer skips the empty black cartridge. Files never leave your device.

Mapping: white stays white (255,255,255) (no ink), black becomes the full ink color, greys become light tints. Only dark inks are offered — blue, red, magenta, dark green — so the page still reads as black/dark-grey if someone photocopies it in B&W. The output PDF keeps the original page size.

How it works & privacy
  1. Your PDF is rasterized with pdf.js at the DPI you pick.
  2. Each pixel's luminance L = 0.299·R + 0.587·G + 0.114·B is computed (same as Pillow's convert("L")).
  3. Each pixel is remapped so white stays white and black becomes the full ink color: out = 255 − (255 − ink)·(255 − L)/255 (for blue ink that's exactly (L, L, 255)).
  4. Pages are re-assembled into a PDF with pdf-lib, preserving each page's physical size in points.

Privacy: no server, no upload — open DevTools → Network and watch: the only requests are the CDN libraries. Note the output is rasterized (text is no longer selectable) — ideal for labels and forms.
