Lesson: why it curves

A car pivots around its rear axle. Front wheels angled = car drives on a circle. More lock = tighter circle. In reverse the rear swings wide — that's why you turn late going in nose-first, and early backing in. Try full lock at crawling speed and watch the dotted arc.
